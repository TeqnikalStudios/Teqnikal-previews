How delivery works

Our own drivers deliver. Every order is age-checked online and again at your door.

    Check out with your name, address and date of birth. We confirm you're 21+ before we accept the order.

    A Smokers Only driver brings your sealed order. You'll get a text when they're on the way.

    The driver scans your ID and you sign. No ID, no handoff. We never leave orders at the door.
