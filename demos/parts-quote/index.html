Why it matters

Quote requests are where distributors win or lose the order.

Speed

First quote back usually wins

Buyers send the same list to three suppliers. A rep keying 12 lines by hand takes 30 to 60 minutes. A draft in seconds means you answer first, even at 6:40 in the morning.

Accuracy

No more wrong part numbers

Every line is checked against your catalog. Typos, cross-references and “brass ball valve, half inch” style descriptions are caught and shown to a person, not guessed.

Control

Your team still decides

Nothing goes out on its own. Clean lines are ready to send, and anything uncertain is flagged with the reason so a rep fixes it in a minute instead of rebuilding the quote.

How it works

It plugs into the inbox and systems you already use.

  1. Email arrives

    A request hits your quotes inbox. TEQNIKAL OS picks it up automatically.

  2. Lines are matched

    Part numbers, quantities and descriptions are pulled out and checked against your catalog and stock.

  3. Pricing is applied

    The sender is matched to their account, so contract discounts and quantity breaks are used.

  4. A rep reviews

    The draft quote and reply land with your team, with flagged lines on top. One review, then send.
